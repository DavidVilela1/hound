# Hound allowlist
# ---------------
# Domains and devices whose risk indicators should not be counted. Use it for things you
# have checked and trust, e.g. a CDN whose random-looking host names trip the entropy
# signal, or your own NAS that legitimately connects to many devices.
# One entry per line. Blank lines and text after '#' are ignored. Restart Hound after
# editing. Nothing is hidden: affected events keep an "ALLOWLISTED" reason that names the
# entry and the indicators it covered.
#
# Domains - match the name itself and every subdomain (like the blocklist):
#   cdn.example.com
#
# Devices - a source IP address, or a CIDR range of addresses:
#   192.168.1.20
#   192.168.1.64/28
#
# Rules:
# * An allowlisted DOMAIN has none of its indicators counted, including a blocklist match
#   on that exact name: you have vouched for it.
# * An allowlisted DEVICE has its behaviour ignored (scans, repeated attempts, unusual
#   ports, ...), but a BLOCKLIST HIT STILL COUNTS: trusting a device must not hide it
#   contacting a known-bad domain.
# * Single words such as "com" are rejected. Ranges wider than /24 (IPv4) or /64 (IPv6)
#   are accepted but logged as a warning.
